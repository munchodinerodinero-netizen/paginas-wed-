# SHORT EN-S03 — "It's Not Unemployment. It's Leverage."

**Video origen:** EN-L01
**Tipo:** SHORT G — Cliffhanger / giro narrativo
**Duración:** ~55s

## 0-2s — HOOK
> Everyone's asking "will AI take my job?" Wrong question.

*(Visual: text "WRONG QUESTION" stamped over a generic "AI replacing jobs" headline)*

## 2-5s — CONTEXTO
> Law firms using AI aren't hiring fewer lawyers because they need less
> legal work done.

## 5-25s — VALOR
> They're hiring fewer because one lawyer with AI tools can now do what
> used to take a team of five. The work didn't disappear. The headcount
> needed to do it did.
>
> That's leverage — and leverage always does the same two things: it
> makes the people at the top far more productive, and it deletes the
> entry-level version of the job that used to train the next generation.

*(Visual: org chart animation — top layer stays, base layer shrinks)*

## 25-40s — PAYOFF
> This is the actual pattern behind every "AI took the job" headline —
> radiology, financial analysis, customer support, now law. Not
> extinction. Compression.

## 40-55s — CTA
> The real question isn't "will AI take my job." It's "when the
> headcount for my job drops by 80%, am I in the 20% that's left?"
> That's what we're mapping out on this channel — subscribe.
