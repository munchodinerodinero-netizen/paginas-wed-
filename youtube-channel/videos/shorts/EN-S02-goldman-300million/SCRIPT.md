# SHORT EN-S02 — "300 Million Jobs"

**Video origen:** EN-L01
**Tipo:** SHORT B — Momento polémico / dato de escala
**Duración:** ~45s

## 0-2s — HOOK
> Goldman Sachs put a number on it: 300 million jobs.

*(Visual: bold on-screen text "300,000,000 JOBS" with slow zoom)*

## 2-5s — CONTEXTO
> Not fired. Not "at risk someday." Exposed to AI automation — starting now.

## 5-25s — VALOR
> In 2023, Goldman Sachs estimated that generative AI could eventually
> automate tasks equivalent to 300 million full-time jobs worldwide.
>
> And the professions ranked highest for exposure weren't factory jobs.
> They were document-heavy, judgment-light knowledge work — lawyers,
> analysts, paralegals, researchers. The jobs people spent a decade and
> six figures in debt training for.

*(Visual: icons of briefcase/scale/magnifying glass fading into AI chip icon)*

## 25-40s — PAYOFF
> Here's the part nobody says out loud: it's not about the whole
> profession disappearing. It's about needing one-tenth the people to
> do the same amount of work — and the entry-level jobs are the ones
> that vanish first.

## 40-45s — CTA
> If your job involves reading, summarizing, or reviewing documents at
> scale — this is already about you. Follow to see which industry is
> next.
