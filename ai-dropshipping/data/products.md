## ⭐ SHORTLISTED WINNER — Portable Garment Steamer

- **Score: 74/100** (Trend 16/20, Demand 15/20, Low competition 9/15,
  Margin 15/20 *estimate*, Supplier reliability 12/15 *estimate*, Wow factor 8/10)
- **Trend direction:** Actively viral on TikTok under #tiktokmademebuyit and
  #PortableSteamer; framed by multiple 2026 trend roundups as a top pick for
  the category. ([productlair.com](https://productlair.com/collections/trending-tiktok), [SlashGear](https://www.slashgear.com/1790863/instagram-tiktok-clothing-steamer-tested/))
- **Demand signals:** Multiple active listings on TikTok Shop (Mecheer,
  others) and mainstream retail (Walmart, Slickdeals deal on a Clevast unit
  at $14.99) — demand extends beyond the TikTok fad into an established
  product category, which is a durability signal most viral gadgets don't have.
  ([TikTok Shop listing](https://shop.tiktok.com/us/pdp/mecheer-portable-travel-garment-steamer-fast-heating/1732300670527115751), [Walmart](https://www.slashgear.com/1790863/instagram-tiktok-clothing-steamer-tested/))
- **Competition level:** Moderate — commoditized on marketplaces (TikTok Shop,
  Walmart, Amazon) with several no-name sellers, but **no dominant standalone
  branded DTC store found** in this search — that's a real gap, not a guess
  (see `competitors.md`).
- **Est. selling price:** $25–$35 (estimate, based on comparable retail range
  $18–$32 cited above, positioned slightly premium with branding)
- **Est. supplier cost:** $6–$10 (estimate — exact AliExpress/CJ wholesale
  tier price wasn't available without logging into a supplier account; verify
  before committing)
- **Est. margin:** ~$18–$25 / ~65–70% (estimate, before ad spend)
- **Supplier reliability:** Simple electronics (heating element + water tank,
  no battery) — lower shipping/compliance risk than battery-powered gadgets.
  Widely available across AliExpress/CJ Dropshipping (estimate — not vetted
  with an actual sample order yet, per system rule that stays human-only).
- **Notes:** Category is more durable than a pure TikTok fad because
  garment steamers already sell year-round outside of trend cycles.

## Considered + rejected

### Rotating "SpinSpice"-style 360° spice/cosmetics organizer

- **Score: 79/100 on paper → downgraded, not recommended**
  (Trend 17/20, Demand 14/20, Low competition **2/15 — corrected down**,
  Margin 17/20 *estimate cited 40–58%*, Supplier reliability 10/15 *estimate*,
  Wow factor 9/10)
- **Why it scores high on paper but is a bad pick:** direct search turned up
  10+ near-identical "unbranded"/generic listings already live on TikTok
  Shop for this exact product ([TikTok Shop search](https://shop.tiktok.com/us/k/spice-rack-organizer)), plus a **$1.25–$1.50 Dollar Tree
  version already viral on TikTok** as a budget alternative
  ([TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/discover/dollar-tree-kitchen-spices-organizer)). A solo operator can't out-brand a $1.25 in-store
  alternative or a dozen identical $8 TikTok Shop listings — there's no real
  gap left to own. **Killed, not shortlisted.**

### Portable tire inflator / jump starter (2-in-1)

- **Score: 67/100 — not recommended for a first solo store**
  (Trend 18/20, Demand 17/20, Low competition 6/15, Margin 10/20 *estimate*,
  Supplier reliability 9/15 *estimate*, Wow factor 7/10)
- **Why not:** dominated by established branded competitors already at
  national retail (GOOLOO A3, Hieha, Avapow — all cited with real prices
  $40–$110) ([Yahoo/Walmart](https://tech.yahoo.com/home/articles/walmart-2-1-tire-inflator-200000578.html)). Contains a lithium battery — real
  added complexity for a first product: shipping restrictions (most couriers
  require UN38.3 battery documentation), higher defect/return risk, and
  higher liability if it fails. Wrong pick for a first solo run.
