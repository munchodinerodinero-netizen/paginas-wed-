# Supplier Database

Product: **Portable Garment Steamer**. Real, cited signals only — no
invented prices, ratings, or supplier names. This search hit a real limit:
exact wholesale tier pricing and supplier ratings live behind AliExpress/CJ
Dropshipping's own logged-in interface, which isn't reachable from here.
**That check stays a human task before any sample order** — see below.

## What's confirmed from open search

- **Market availability:** AliExpress carries multiple active listings under
  "portable garment steamer" / "handheld garment steamer" / "mini garment
  steamer," described as low-MOQ, direct-from-factory. Exact per-unit price
  varies by listing and wasn't visible without logging in.
  ([AliExpress category](https://www.aliexpress.com/w/wholesale-portable-garment-steamer.html), [Global Sources wholesale](https://www.globalsources.com/category/garment-steamers-for-sale-price_23432/))
- **CJ Dropshipping:** platform confirmed to carry general appliance
  sourcing with Shopify/WooCommerce integration; standard international
  shipping cited at 7–17 days via CJ's own transit lines, or 3–7 days if
  routed through one of their in-country warehouses (relevant if a US/MX
  warehouse option exists for this SKU — confirm directly).
  ([CJ shipping guide](https://blog.cjdropshipping.com/detail/shipping-price-and-delivery-time/))
- **One real retail cost anchor:** a comparable 800W portable handheld
  garment steamer (Vavsea brand) was found retailing at **$12.95** with free
  shipping (Walmart, via a Slickdeals-tracked deal, "35 orders" noted on the
  deal post). ([Slickdeals](https://slickdeals.net/f/18974341-vavsea-800w-portable-handheld-garment-steamer-12-95-free-shipping-w-walmart-or-35-orders))
  This is a **retail** price, not a supplier's wholesale price — but it puts
  a ceiling on what the finished unit costs to make: if it retails at
  $12.95 with margin for Walmart + the seller, a wholesale/dropship unit
  cost in the **$4–$9** range (already used as the estimate in
  `products.md`) is realistic, not just guessed from nothing.

## What's NOT confirmed — human task before sourcing a sample

- [ ] Exact wholesale unit cost at your order volume (needs a logged-in
      AliExpress/CJ account — price tiers aren't public).
- [ ] Specific supplier rating / order-count / dispute history for whichever
      exact listing you pick — this is the single most important filter and
      it has to be checked by a person, on the platform, before ordering.
- [ ] Real shipping time to Mexico specifically (the CJ numbers above are
      general, not MX-specific).
- [ ] Sample order and hands-on quality check — **non-negotiable before this
      goes live**, per `CLAUDE.md`: "Order a sample yourself (check quality
      and shipping time)."

## How to qualify a specific listing (once you're logged in)

1. Filter by "Free Shipping" + 4.5★+ rating + 500+ orders as a floor.
2. Compare 3 listings at the same spec tier (wattage, tank size) — price
   variance between suppliers for an identical unit is normal; pick on
   reliability signals, not just lowest price.
3. Message the supplier directly about MOQ and real shipping time to Mexico
   before ordering the sample.
4. Order **one sample yourself.** No supplier gets added here as "vetted"
   without that step done by a human.
