# Ad Concepts

Product: **Portable Garment Steamer** ("Pressly" working brand). Built from
`brand.md` + `competitors.md`. **Concepts only — a human produces
(CapCut/Canva) and launches. No fabricated proof/stats. Nothing here spends
a dollar until Alberto approves it and launches it himself.**

Every script avoids stating specs not yet verified against a real sourced
unit (heat-up time, wattage) — see the open items in `brand.md`. Claims stay
qualitative ("fast," "in seconds") until a sample is tested.

---

## Concept 1 — Problem/solution demo

- **Hook (0–2s):** "POV: you're about to walk into a meeting looking like you
  slept in your shirt."
- **Script:** Hook → show visibly wrinkled shirt → grab steamer, plug in →
  press against fabric, wrinkles release on camera → "Wrinkle-free in
  seconds. No board, no burnt shirts." → CTA: "Link in bio."
- **Format:** Demo, phone camera, no voiceover needed — visual carries it.
- **CTA:** "Get yours before your next meeting."

## Concept 2 — Desire: travel/pack light

- **Hook:** "The one thing I never travel without anymore."
- **Script:** Hook → show it fitting in a small bag pocket → hotel room
  scene, steaming a shirt in under a minute → "No ironing board in a hotel
  room. Ever again." → CTA.
- **Format:** UGC, travel setting.
- **CTA:** "Pack it. Never iron in a hotel again."

## Concept 3 — Before/after

- **Hook:** "Same shirt. 30 seconds apart." *(claim: relative, not an exact
  timed number until verified — swap in the real tested time before
  launch.)*
- **Script:** Split-screen or quick-cut: wrinkled shirt → steamed shirt,
  no talking, on-screen text carries it → CTA at the end.
- **Format:** Static/quick-cut demo, captioned for sound-off viewing.
- **CTA:** "See it work → link in bio."

## Concept 4 — Testimonial

- **Status:** ⏸️ **Held until a real customer exists.** Concept reserved for
  a genuine unboxing/reaction clip from an actual buyer — do not script fake
  dialogue or produce this with an actor reading invented lines.
- **Format (once real):** UGC reaction, unscripted, real voice.

## Concept 5 — Curiosity: "3 things you didn't know you could steam"

- **Hook:** "3 things in your closet you're not steaming (but should be)."
- **Script:** Hook → quick demo on 3 items (suit jacket, curtains, bedsheet
  corner) → "One tool, way more than shirts." → CTA.
- **Format:** Demo, fast cuts, numbered on-screen text.
- **CTA:** "Try it on everything."

## Concept 6 — Convenience vs. ironing board

- **Hook:** "I haven't touched my ironing board in 3 months."
- **Script:** Hook → show ironing board folded up/in storage/collecting
  dust → cut to steamer in use, done in seconds → "Same result. Way less
  effort." → CTA.
- **Format:** UGC, relatable home setting.
- **CTA:** "Retire your ironing board."

## Concept 7 — Honest positioning vs. the $15 throwaway tier

- **Hook:** "Why I stopped buying the $15 steamer every 2 months."
- **Script:** Hook → state plainly: cheap steamers are cheap because they're
  built to be replaced, not because they're a better deal → position Pressly
  as the one you buy once → CTA.
- **Format:** Talking-head / voiceover + product shots.
- **CTA:** "Buy it once."
- **Guardrail:** No naming competitor brands, no claiming a specific failure
  rate or lifespan number we haven't tested — keep it a values statement
  ("built to be replaced" is a category critique, not a claim about a named
  product), not a fabricated stat.

## Concept 8 — Honest positioning vs. the $65–75 premium tier

- **Hook:** "You don't need to spend $75 to steam a shirt."
- **Script:** Hook → "The $75 ones have more wattage you'll never notice in
  a 30-second steam session. You're paying for a name." → show Pressly doing
  the same job → CTA with price shown.
- **Format:** Talking-head or split-screen price comparison graphic.
- **CTA:** "Same result, half the price."
- **Guardrail:** No specific competitor brand names in the ad itself (ad
  policy risk + unverified performance-parity claim) — keep it category-level
  ("premium brands"), not "beats Rowenta."

## Concept 9 — UGC morning routine

- **Hook:** "My actual morning routine before work."
- **Script:** Quick montage — coffee, outfit laid out, 10-second steam pass,
  out the door → text overlay: "the one step that used to take 10 minutes."
- **Format:** UGC, day-in-the-life.
- **CTA:** "Get yours."

## Concept 10 — Parent/quick-fix

- **Hook:** "5 minutes before we're supposed to leave and his shirt looks
  like this."
- **Script:** Hook → chaos-relatable shot of wrinkled kid's outfit → quick
  steam fix → out the door on time → CTA.
- **Format:** UGC, parent-relatable, light humor.
- **CTA:** "The fix that saves your morning."
