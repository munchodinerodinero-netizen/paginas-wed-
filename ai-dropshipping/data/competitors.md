# Competitor Research

Product: **Portable Garment Steamer** (from `products.md`). Real, cited
listings only — no invented reviews or numbers.

## Landscape — two clusters, real gap in the middle

### Cluster 1 — Budget marketplace commodity ($15–$30)

- **CLEVAST Travel Steamer** — $14.99 (deal price, regular ~$30), 1000W,
  ceramic plate, 30s heat-up, 120ml tank / ~7 min run time. Sold on
  Amazon/Target/via deal sites. Feedback found is positive but thin ("results
  come out perfect," good for travel) — no volume of reviews confirmed.
  ([Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/CLEVAST-Handheld-Steamer-Foldable-Portable/dp/B0D8B8ZF3T), [Slickdeals](https://slickdeals.net/f/19306560-clevast-1000w-portable-garment-steamer-14-99), [Target](https://www.target.com/p/clevast-travel-steamer-for-clothes-portable-handheld-garment-steamer-for-clothing/-/A-1005825643))
- **HiLIFE Portable Steamer** — under $30, cited with 100,000+ positive
  Amazon ratings — the volume leader in the budget tier.
  ([itechguides.com](https://www.itechguides.com/the-8-best-travel-steamers-of-2026-tested-and-reviewed/))
- **Generic TikTok Shop sellers** (Mecheer and others, unbranded listings) —
  present but thin on differentiation; promos/discount-driven (e.g. "Dr.
  Flash Garment Steamer at 60% off").
- **Store style / offer:** marketplace listing pages, price-led, no brand
  story, no guarantee beyond platform return policy.
- **Complaints:** not confirmed in this search — flagging that as a gap in
  the data, not a claim of "no complaints exist."

### Cluster 2 — Established premium brands ($65–$75)

- **Rowenta X-Cel DR8120** — $74.99, 1600W, 40s heat-up, dual delicate/turbo
  settings. Cited as "best garment steamer of 2026" by one reviewer.
  ([theconsumers.guide](https://www.theconsumers.guide/reviews/best-garment-steamers-handheld-professional-2026))
- **Conair Turbo ExtremeSteam GS59X** — best-overall pick, 1875W, strongest
  steam output in the handheld category.
  ([hgtv.com](https://www.hgtv.com/shopping/product-reviews/best-clothes-steamers))
- **Jack & Rose** — $65.99 on Amazon, also promoted via TikTok Shop with
  coupon discounts.
- **Store style / offer:** established retail brand pages, real long-form
  reviews, brand trust already built — hard to out-brand directly.

## The gap (what LYON-style positioning would look like — not copying either cluster)

Nobody found in this search sits in the **$25–$35 "trustworthy mid-tier"**
band with an actual brand behind it: budget tier ($15–$30) is commodity/
promo-driven with thin trust signals; premium tier ($65–$75) is
brand-trusted but priced 2–3x higher for marginal wattage/feature gains a
casual buyer won't notice in a 15-second demo video.

**The opening:** a single, well-branded product with an honest guarantee
(real return policy, not vague), a demo-first product page (before/after,
how-it-works), and a price that undercuts the premium tier by half while
looking meaningfully more trustworthy than the $15 commodity listings. Same
logic Alberto uses for LYON against $20K-peso agencies vs. $0 DIY tools:
sit in the credible middle, not the cheapest or the most expensive.

## What we will NOT do

- Not copying Rowenta/Conair copy, imagery, or claims.
- Not fabricating reviews, ratings counts, or "as seen on" badges — real
  social proof only, added once it exists.
- Not claiming performance specs (wattage, heat-up time) we haven't verified
  on our own sourced unit.
