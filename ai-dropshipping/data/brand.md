# Brand Brief + Product Page Draft

Product: **Portable Garment Steamer**. Built from `products.md` +
`competitors.md`. **Draft only — requires human approval before publishing.**
No fake claims, reviews, or testimonials.

## Brand name ideas (5)

1. **Pressly** — clean, modern, implies "press instantly"
2. **SteamKit** — plain, descriptive, easy to trust
3. **Crease.co** — playful "the enemy of creases," short domain-friendly
4. **VelvetPress** — premium-leaning, textile-word association
5. **QuickPress Co.** — leads directly with the core benefit (speed)

*(Working pick for the draft below: **Pressly** — short, brandable, easy to
say in a 3-second TikTok hook. Swap freely — this is not the decision that
matters most; the offer and gap are.)*

## Positioning

> "The steamer that costs half of what the premium brands charge, and looks
> nothing like a $15 throwaway."

Sits deliberately in the gap identified in `competitors.md`: not the
cheapest, not the most expensive — the one a buyer trusts enough to actually
click "buy" without doing 20 minutes of review research first.

## Brand voice

- Direct, demo-first, no fluff — the product sells itself in an 8-second
  clip, the copy should get out of the way fast.
- Confident about the one thing that matters (fast, no more travel-wrinkled
  clothes) — doesn't over-claim specs we haven't verified ourselves.
- Mobile-first tone: short sentences, benefit-first headlines.

## Product page draft

**Headline:** Wrinkle-free clothes in 30 seconds. Anywhere.

**Subhead:** The portable steamer that skips the ironing board — for travel,
work, and mornings you're already running late.

**Hero benefit:** No ironing board. No burnt shirts. No waiting 10 minutes
for a plate to heat up.

**3 benefit blocks:**
1. **Fast** — heats up in seconds, not minutes *(exact heat-up time to be
   confirmed once a sample unit is sourced and tested — do not publish an
   unverified number)*.
2. **Portable** — fits in a bag; steam a shirt in a hotel room, backstage,
   or before a meeting.
3. **Safe on fabric** — gentle steam instead of direct-heat ironing, safer
   for delicate fabrics *(claim to verify against the actual sourced unit's
   spec sheet before publishing)*.

**How it works:** Fill tank → plug in → press against fabric → wrinkles
release in seconds. (3-step visual, demo-video-first section — this is the
highest-converting section per the competitor research; TikTok reviewers
lead with the demo, we should too.)

**Social proof:** *Placeholder — empty until real customer photos/reviews
exist. Do not fill with invented testimonials.* Once first orders ship,
Customer Support agent + a manual review-request flow populate this.

**FAQ:**
- Is it safe for [fabric type]? *(Answer once verified against real spec
  sheet.)*
- How long does the water tank last? *(Answer once verified.)*
- What's the warranty/return policy? *(Alberto/human sets an honest, real
  policy before this goes live — never leave vague.)*
- How fast does it ship? *(Set real, honest shipping-time expectations —
  see `CLAUDE.md` error-handling: honest delivery expectations prevent
  chargebacks.)*

**Offer + guarantee:** Single unit price positioned at $25–$35 (see
`products.md` estimate). Guarantee copy: *"[N]-day money-back guarantee"* —
**N is a business decision for the human to set, not invented here.**

**Upsell:** Replacement water-tank cap / travel pouch / 2-pack discount —
sourced only once confirmed available from the same supplier.

**Sticky CTA (mobile):** "Get yours — $[price]" — persistent bottom bar on
mobile, per template.

## Checkout structure (mobile-first)

1. Product page → sticky "Buy now" bar visible on scroll.
2. One-page checkout: shipping → payment (no forced account creation).
3. Order confirmation page offers the upsell once, no pop-up spam.
4. Post-purchase: automated "order confirmed" email/SMS only — no marketing
   claims not already on the product page.
